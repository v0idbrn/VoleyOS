VolleyOS is the offline-first operating system for volleyball. Eighteen modules that work in a gym with no signal, on a phone, tablet or laptop.

WHAT YOU GET
• 5-Frame Lab: measure jump height and attack mechanics with physics, not guesswork.
• 5-1 Board: validate every rotation R1-R6 against the FIVB overlap rule at serve contact.
• 3-Click Scouting: type or dictate a play, get side-out by rotation and a 3x3 heatmap.
• Live Scoreboard: indoor scoring with automatic rotation and set/match logic.
• Tournament: standings with 3-2-1-0 points and set/point ratios.
• Pickup Balancer: fair teams with one setter each, plus king-of-the-court queue.
• OBS Overlay: transparent browser source with set point and match point banners.
• Pocket VAR: plain-language rule checks with FIVB citations and a coin toss.
• Beach: 21/15 scoring, side switches every 7/5 points, server tracking.
• Volleycards: hexagonal player radar with spike and block reach.
• Attack Tempo: 1st, 2nd, pipe and 3rd tempo windows in milliseconds.
• ACWR Monitor: acute:chronic jump load with fatigue and injury-risk zones.
• Block Shadow: shadow cone, defender ejection and court coverage.
• Seam Radar: receiver seam owner, priority and a drill to close the gap.
• Setter IQ: R1-R6 distribution, 0-100 unpredictability and pass-quality bias.
• Lineup Slip: FIVB I-VI sheet with pairs three positions apart and libero windows.
• Integrity Seal: HMAC-SHA256 over canonical JSON to detect altered match sheets.
• .volleyos Capsule: export and import boards, rosters and tournaments with a SHA-256 checksum.

BUILT FOR REAL COURTS
• 100% offline. No accounts, no ads, no telemetry.
• Spanish, English and Portuguese.
• Ephemeral vault: reports stay in local storage and can be destroyed with one tap, with a UTC receipt.
• Excel exports with an audit note for every calculation.
• Security shield: input sanitisation, formula-injection protection, 256 KiB body cap and rate limiting on the API.
• Free software under AGPL-3.0-or-later. No proprietary SDKs.

CLOUD PRO (OPTIONAL)
Clubs that stream or run leagues can buy one-time event passes for multi-court tournaments, white-label overlays, API access and HMAC-signed payment webhooks. The free app is never locked.

VolleyOS does not replace the official referee or a doctor. VAR and rotation checks are advisory; biomechanics and load metrics are educational.
